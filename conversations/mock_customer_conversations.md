# Mock Customer Conversations

> All names, customers, and scenarios are fictional role-play exercises. These are practice examples, not real support transcripts.

## Scenario 1: Candidate import is blocked

**Customer:** I uploaded our candidate CSV twice, and I still cannot find the imported records. We need them for today's shortlist.

**Associate:** I understand the shortlist is time-sensitive. Let's verify the import result before trying it again, so we don't create duplicates. Did the import show successful, skipped, and failed counts? Please share the non-sensitive error text and the approximate time of the latest attempt—no candidate details are needed.

**Customer:** It says 18 rows failed because of a field mapping error.

**Associate:** Thanks. Please compare the column headings against the current template and check whether the required fields have exactly matching names. We can test one or two synthetic rows first. If those still fail, I'll document the exact message and escalate with a sanitised example rather than asking you to re-upload the full file repeatedly.

**Associate should demonstrate:** empathy, impact clarification, duplicate prevention, safe data handling, and a clear next step.

## Scenario 2: Frustrated user cannot see a job

**Customer:** Your system has lost my job opening. This is the third time I've had a problem.

**Associate:** I'm sorry this is disrupting your work, especially after previous issues. Let's check the job status, owner, filters, and workspace first. Is the job missing for you only, or can your teammates not see it either?

**Customer:** My teammate can see it; I can't.

**Associate:** That helps narrow it down to a possible view or access difference rather than assuming the job is lost. Please confirm you're in the right workspace and clear any active filters. If it is still missing, I'll check the access path with an authorised admin and keep you updated. I won't change permissions without approval.

**Associate should demonstrate:** de-escalation, no defensiveness, one focused question, safe permission handling.

## Scenario 3: Report total looks wrong

**Customer:** The weekly report says 42 candidates, but my pipeline view shows 37. Your numbers are unreliable.

**Associate:** I can see why that would affect your confidence in the report. Let's compare the date range, time zone, stage filters, and whether archived records are included in each view. Could you confirm those settings for both screens? We can then compare a small sample and identify whether the difference is from the report definition or a potential defect.

**Customer:** The report includes archived candidates.

**Associate:** That may explain the difference, but let's verify the count after applying the same filter. I'll document the definition so your team can reproduce the report consistently. If the totals still disagree under matching settings, I'll escalate with the exact filters and examples.

**Associate should demonstrate:** avoid guessing, reconcile definitions, explain clearly, and escalate only with reproducible evidence.

## Practice scoring rubric
Score each item from 1 (needs work) to 5 (strong):
- Acknowledges customer impact without blame.
- Asks focused questions before offering a fix.
- Explains actions in plain language.
- Avoids unsupported promises and unsafe handling of data.
- States the next step, owner, and update expectation.
- Confirms resolution and documents learnings.
