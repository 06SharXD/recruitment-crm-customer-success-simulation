# Customer Onboarding Guide — Fictional Recruitment CRM

> **Simulation:** This guide is for a fictional recruitment CRM workflow. It is not official Recruit CRM documentation.

## Objective
Help a new recruitment-agency team understand the core workflow, configure access safely, and form a repeatable daily habit.

## Before the first session
- Identify the agency administrator and the users who need access.
- Clarify team structure, recruitment stages, reporting goals, and current data sources.
- Confirm what candidate/client data is approved for import and who owns data quality.
- Agree on the first success outcome (for example, one test job and a small test candidate import).

## Suggested onboarding sequence

### 1. Workspace and users
1. Confirm workspace identity and primary administrator.
2. Add only authorised users and assign least-privilege roles.
3. Ask each user to verify access and notification preferences.
4. Do not ask customers to share passwords or authentication codes.

### 2. Configure recruitment workflow
1. Create a sample job with a clear title, client, owner, and status.
2. Agree on interview stages that match the agency's process.
3. Demonstrate how candidate and client records relate to a job.
4. Review how closed or archived records should be handled.

### 3. Import a small sample
1. Download the agreed template.
2. Standardise column names, dates, and required fields.
3. Remove duplicate test records and confirm the lawful basis/permission for data use.
4. Import a small sample before the full migration.
5. Reconcile source count against successful, skipped, and failed rows.

### 4. Adoption and reporting
- Demonstrate search, saved filters, tasks/reminders, and basic reporting.
- Ask the user to complete a task while sharing their screen or narrating their steps.
- Agree on a short follow-up after the first week.
- Track adoption questions and blockers; do not assume login count alone means value.

## First-week success checklist
- [ ] Admin and intended users can access the workspace.
- [ ] A test job and agreed recruitment stages exist.
- [ ] A small sample import was reconciled.
- [ ] Users can find a candidate and associate a candidate with a job.
- [ ] Notification and time-zone settings were checked.
- [ ] The team knows how to ask for help and what information to include.
- [ ] Follow-up date and success criteria are agreed.

## Communication template
**Subject:** Your recruitment workspace — next steps

Hi [Name],

Thanks for walking through your recruitment workflow with me. Our agreed next step is [specific action]. Please try it with [small, safe sample] and let me know where the steps differ from your process. If you encounter an issue, please share the action you took, the result you expected, the result you saw, and the approximate time it happened—without sending passwords or unnecessary candidate personal data.

I'll follow up on [date] to review progress and any blockers.

Best,
[Your name]

## Escalation note
For suspected exposure of candidate data, unauthorised access, or data loss, stop the risky action and follow the organisation's security escalation policy immediately. Do not use real personal data in a demo or portfolio project.
