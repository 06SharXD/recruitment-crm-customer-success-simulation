# Role Alignment and Sources

**Project:** Recruitment CRM Customer Success & Support Simulation  
**Prepared:** 29 October 2026

## How the project maps to a customer success associate role

| Role-related skill area | Portfolio evidence | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Respond to customer questions | Mock conversations and FAQ | Written role-play is not real customer handling. |
| Explain features and workarounds | Onboarding guide and scenario responses | Confirm actual product behaviour against up-to-date official documentation. |
| Troubleshoot and escalate | Ticket records and support playbook | Synthetic incidents do not prove performance in a live queue. |
| Build customer relationships | Follow-up and onboarding patterns | Real relationship evidence must come from genuine activities such as partner coordination. |
| Organise service delivery | Dashboard, status, priority, and next-action fields | Sample metrics are illustrative and are not business achievements. |

## Honest presentation checklist

- Call the work a **simulation** or **portfolio project**.
- Describe the 32 records as synthetic examples.
- Do not claim professional customer support, actual customer onboarding, or a real CSAT increase.
- Do not imply the prototype is the real Recruit CRM product.
- Explain your personal decisions and test the demo before sharing it.
