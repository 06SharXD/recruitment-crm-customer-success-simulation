Support operating principles

  1. Confirm the user's goal and impact before proposing a fix.
  2. Set priority by urgency, impact, and scope—not by tone alone.
  3. Provide a clear next step and realistic expectation; never promise an unverified fix.
  4. Escalate security, data-loss, widespread outage, and repeated failures.
  5. Document the cause, workaround, owner, and follow-up.
